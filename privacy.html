Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

At Arizona AI, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the controls you have. We believe privacy is a right — not a feature.

Your data is yours. We use Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) at the database level. This means even our engineers cannot read your private messages, drafts, or personal data without your explicit consent.

1 What We Collect

When you use Arizona AI, we collect the minimum amount of information needed to run the service:

  • Account info: Email address, username, and password (encrypted)
  • Profile data: Display name, bio, avatar, location (all optional)
  • Content: Posts, comments, messages, and media you upload
  • Usage data: Posts viewed, likes given, follows, and interactions
  • Device info: Browser type and IP address (for security only)

2 How We Use Your Data

We use your information only to:

  • Provide the core features of the platform
  • Personalize your feed with AI-powered recommendations
  • Calculate your earnings and manage your wallet
  • Send important account and security emails
  • Protect against fraud, spam, and abuse
  • Improve our services and fix bugs

We never sell your data to third parties or advertisers.

3 What We Don't Do

  • ❌ We do not sell your personal information
  • ❌ We do not share your private messages with anyone
  • ❌ We do not track you across other websites
  • ❌ We do not use your content to train third-party AI models
  • ❌ We do not read your DMs unless legally required

4 Data Security

We protect your data using industry-standard practices:

  • Encryption in transit: All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS
  • Encryption at rest: Database and storage are encrypted
  • Row-Level Security: Users can only access their own data
  • Password hashing: We never store passwords in plain text
  • Regular backups: Your data is backed up securely

5 Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can:

  • Access — Download a copy of your data anytime
  • Correct — Edit your profile info at any time
  • Delete — Delete individual posts or your entire account
  • Export — Request a full data export
  • Opt out — Disable non-essential emails

To exercise any of these rights, visit your Profile Settings or contact our support team.

6 Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed:

  • Active accounts: Data retained while you use the platform
  • Deleted posts: Removed immediately from public view
  • Deleted accounts: Fully removed within 30 days
  • Backups: Purged after 90 days
  • Legal holds: May extend retention if required by law

7 Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies for essential functionality only:

  • Session cookies: To keep you logged in
  • Preference cookies: To remember your language choice
  • Security cookies: To protect against CSRF attacks

We do not use third-party tracking, advertising cookies, or analytics that identify you personally.

8 Children's Privacy

Arizona AI is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it immediately.

If you are between 13 and 18, please use the platform with parental consent.

9 Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted services to run the platform:

  • Supabase — Database, authentication, and file storage
  • Resend — Transactional email delivery
  • GitHub Pages — Website hosting

Each service has its own privacy policy and is contractually bound to protect your data.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do:

  • We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top
  • We'll notify you via email if changes are significant
  • Continued use of the platform means you accept the changes

11 Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws applicable to Arizona AI's operations. Any disputes will be handled through good-faith negotiation before legal action.
