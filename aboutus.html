Our Story

Arizona AI is the flagship platform of Huzni's Family, an ambitious initiative to merge artificial intelligence with social media in a way that rewards creators directly — not just the platforms they use.

Our journey begins with Galab Al-Barak Co. — a well-established family company with deep roots in premium goods. Under the Arizona Oil brand, Galab Al-Barak has built a reputation for quality, trust, and family-driven values.

Led by our Executive Director, Masood Huzni, we saw an opportunity to bring those same values — quality, trust, and family — into the digital world. Arizona AI is the result: a social platform where your time and creativity have real value.

🏜️ Our Promise

Every post you make earns money. Every interaction grows your audience. Every day, you build something that's truly yours — on a platform built by a family, for families worldwide.
